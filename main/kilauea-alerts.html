Products / Kilauea Alerts

Kilauea Alerts.

Independent Android companion for Hawaiʻi Island and Kīlauea — dashboards, earthquakes, weather, live official feeds, USGS volcano notices, and NWS alerts in one place.

Android — closed testing on Google Play

Kilauea Alerts is available through Google Play closed testing. If your testers use a Google Group, join the beta tester group first, then open the Play opt-in link on the device where you want the app (same Google account as the Play Store), accept the test, and install or update from the Play Store.

Join closed testing

The running build is shown under About in the app.

What it covers

Official-sourced context, calmer layout.

●

Home & status

Kīlauea-focused dashboard and quick reads so you are not piecing together half a dozen sites.

●

Earthquakes & weather

Recent earthquakes, island-area forecasts, saved locations, and optional GPS for detail screens.

●

Live feeds

Official cameras and streams open in-app so you can watch without juggling bookmarks.

●

USGS & NWS alerts

Volcano notices and weather alerts surfaced together with refresh and notification options where enabled.

●

On your device

Caches and preferences stay on your phone. USGS and NWS are queried from the device; optional weather dashboard summaries may use api.rootrecord.info with an anonymous guest identifier.

●

Independent app

Not produced or endorsed by the USGS or any government agency. Data comes from public official sources; in any emergency, follow civil defense and official instructions.
