Principles in practice

Why people choose RootRecord.

●

On your devices

Business Manager on Android keeps your signed-in workspace with RootRecord’s API; export and backups follow the paths documented in the app. Weather Manager keeps working data on your Android device unless you enable features that sync online.

●

Works in the real world

Day-to-day work is not blocked when the network is spotty. Sign-in, sync, and cloud backup layer on when you enable them.

●

Honest totals

Hours worked, categories, exports — numbers reconcile by design. No surprises when it's time for reports and invoices.

●

Subscription & online

Subscription, sync, and secure online backup when you want them. One account across RootRecord apps where supported.

●

Regular updates

APKs and installers ship on GitHub Releases per product and platform. Each build is noted so you always know what's running.

●

Lifetime option

One payment for ongoing access to current and future RootRecord programs — so the tool grows with your work.
