What you see in the app

Participating apps may show a single line such as Beta Tester Rewards : followed by a number. That is your current usage reward balance for the program. Tap the row to open this page for full context.

How it works (summary)

Rewards are earned while you are signed in and using the app in good faith. The system measures session activity (including time in different parts of the app) and may apply per-screen limits so the program stays fair. Daily earning limits apply per app (each participating app has its own cap for the calendar day), so using more than one app can add up to more than a single app’s limit. Your balance in the app is your running total across the program. From time to time a one-time daily check-in bonus may be available in each app; the app will show if you have already claimed it for that app today.

Exact rates, caps, and eligibility can change during the beta. We will keep this page updated when something material changes.

Your account

Reward balances are associated with the same email you use to sign in on Account. If you have billing or subscription questions, that is separate from this rewards program—see Billing when you need to manage a paid plan.

Custodial wallet & withdrawal fees

On Solana Tools → Account, RootRecord may host a custodial wallet for your rewards. RootRecord pays Solana network fees when you withdraw earned RRTT from that custodial wallet to your linked or saved payout address. You pay on-chain fees when you move native SOL (from whatever balance you keep above the small SOL reserve on the account), USDC, or any other SPL token from that same custodial wallet.

When RRTT and SOL land in your RootRecord Wallet

Usage rewards are tracked on RootRecord first. Moving RRTT from what we owe you into your RootRecord Wallet on Solana is done by an automated backend job that runs once per day at 07:00 UTC. The same pass can add a small amount of SOL to that wallet when the balance is below our internal reserve (so sponsored RRTT cash-outs can still clear). This is not instant: until that daily run completes successfully on-chain, your balance may show credits that are still settling into the wallet. Delays can happen if the network is busy, treasury inventory is short, or your account is not eligible for a send in that window.

Schedule reference for readers: 07:00 UTC daily. For technical wording and edge cases, see Solana Tools → Operations → Docs (RootRecord Wallet & rewards) and Tokenomics & markets.
